About Me

Hi, I'm York Zhong. I focus on extended reality (XR), spatial audio, and interactive media design.

My work explores how sound, digital spaces, and interaction can come together. This portfolio includes a VR experience built with Unreal Engine and FMOD, as well as an augmented reality concept for interactive piano learning.
